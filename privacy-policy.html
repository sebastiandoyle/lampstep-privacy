Lampstep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Support

In short: Lampstep has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your likes, saved verses, reading streak and settings stay on your iPhone. The only information that leaves your device relates to subscriptions, which Apple and our purchase provider RevenueCat process.

Who we are

Lampstep ("the app") is published by Sebastian Doyle. Contact: sebastian.doyle@outlook.com.

Information kept on your device

The Bible text (King James Version and World English Bible, both public domain) is bundled in the app. Verses you like or save, your daily verse counts, the minutes the feed was open, your streak, your chosen translation, goal, text size and reminder time are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Subscriptions

If you start a trial or buy Lampstep Pro, Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information such as the app version and country. This is not linked to your name or email and is used only to unlock the features you paid for. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow them, Lampstep schedules local notifications on your device: an optional daily reminder, and a reminder two days before a free trial ends. They are created on your phone. No push server is involved.

Tracking and advertising

Lampstep does not track you across apps or websites, shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

Lampstep does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For subscription records held by RevenueCat, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them. Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or the Australian Privacy Act), you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
