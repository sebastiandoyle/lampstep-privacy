Lampstep: Scroll the Bible · Privacy Policy

Lampstep is a vertical feed of Bible verses. It lets you swipe through Scripture instead of social media, keep a daily streak, and share verses as images.

Common questions

Which translations are included? The King James Version (1769 text) and the World English Bible. Both are public domain and work offline. Switch between them with the chip at the top right of the feed, or in Settings.

How are minutes counted? Lampstep times how long the feed is open on screen. Minutes appear on the Journey tab and on the "Today" cards in the feed.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Lampstep. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I bought Pro on another device. Open Journey, tap the gear icon, then Restore purchases.
